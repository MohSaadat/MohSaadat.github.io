I am a Post-doctoral Scholar at the Department of Medical Physics of the 
[Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center](https://www.mskcc.org/) in New York.
I will be working under [Dr. Anyi Li](https://www.mskcc.org/profile/anyi-li) under a project titled
"AI-based Clinical Workflow Management System".
I recently defended my Ph.D. in Computer Engineering from the University of South Carolina (USC),
where I was advised by [Dr. Sanjib Sur](https://cse.sc.edu/~sur/) who is an Associate Professor
and the PI of the [SyReX Lab](https://syrex.cse.sc.edu/). 
I completed my B.Sc. in Electrical & Electronics Engineering from Bangladesh University 
of Engineering and Technology (BUET).

Research Areas
==============
Wireless sensing, Digital health, Multi-modal sensing, Image processing
